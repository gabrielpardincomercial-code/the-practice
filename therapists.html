Our standards

What every therapist

brings to the room.

Whoever you choose, you can expect the same foundation of skill, care and accountability.

01

Licensed & credentialed

Every therapist holds an active professional license and has completed graduate-level clinical training.

02

Clinical supervision

Our team meets regularly for case consultation and peer review, so your care benefits from many perspectives.

03

Always learning

Ongoing training in evidence-based approaches keeps our work current, thoughtful and effective.

04

Affirming for everyone

LGBTQIA+ affirming and culturally responsive care, rooted in respect for every identity and story.

Choosing a therapist

Questions,

gently answered.

Finding the right person matters. Here’s what people often ask before they reach out.

01

Can I choose my own therapist?

Absolutely. Browse our team and mention anyone you’d like to work with in the booking form — or let us suggest a match based on what you share.

02

What if the fit doesn’t feel right?

That’s completely okay and more common than you’d think. You can switch therapists at any time, no explanation needed — we’ll help you find someone new.

03

Are all of your therapists licensed?

Yes. Every therapist on our team is a licensed mental health professional who takes part in regular clinical supervision and training.

04

Do all therapists offer online sessions?

Most do. Each profile lists the formats that therapist offers, so you can find someone who works in person, online or both.

05

How soon can I start?

Once you submit the booking form, we’ll reach out to arrange your free consultation and share each therapist’s current availability.
