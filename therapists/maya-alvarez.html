Maya has spent the last decade helping partners and families navigate the moments that feel hardest to talk about. Her sessions are warm, direct and grounded in practical tools you can use at home.

How Maya works

Rather than deciding who’s right, Maya helps clients notice the patterns they get stuck in together and the needs underneath them. She works with couples of every kind, at every stage.
