What we believe

Good

therapy

isn’t

about

fixing

you.

It’s

about

understanding

what

you’re

carrying

and

finding

a

way

forward

that

feels

true

to

you.

01

Connection comes first

Research consistently shows the relationship between you and your therapist is one of the strongest predictors of change. We take time to build trust before anything else.

02

Evidence, not guesswork

Our therapists draw on well-researched methods and keep learning, so the support you receive is grounded, thoughtful and effective.

03

You set the direction

Your goals shape the work. We’ll check in regularly, explain our thinking and adjust whenever something isn’t feeling right.

“You don’t have to have the right words. We’ll find them together.”

How we show up

Our commitments

to you.

Affirming for everyone

LGBTQIA+ affirming, anti-racist and welcoming of every body, identity, relationship and family structure.

Trauma-informed

We prioritise safety, choice and pacing, so therapy never feels like something being done to you.

Culturally responsive

Your background, beliefs and lived experience matter. We bring curiosity and humility to understanding them.

Collaborative & transparent

No jargon, no mystery. We’ll share what we notice, why we suggest things and how we’ll measure progress together.

Our methods

The methods

we draw on.

Every person is different, so we don’t follow a single script. Your therapist will thoughtfully blend approaches based on your needs and what feels right for you.

CBT

01

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy

Practical tools for noticing unhelpful thought patterns and trying kinder, more balanced responses.

Often helpful for: Anxiety · Low mood · Stress

ACT

02

Acceptance & Commitment Therapy

Making room for difficult feelings while moving toward the things that matter most to you.

Often helpful for: Burnout · Chronic stress · Life changes

EFT

03

Emotionally Focused Therapy

Understanding the emotional patterns between people and building a more secure connection.

Often helpful for: Couples · Relationships

PDT

04

Psychodynamic Therapy

Exploring how earlier experiences shape the way you think, feel and relate to others today.

Often helpful for: Recurring patterns · Self-understanding

FST

05

Family Systems Therapy

Looking at how the whole family interacts, so change is shared rather than resting on one person.

Often helpful for: Teens · Parenting · Family conflict

MBT

06

Mindfulness-Based Therapy

Gentle practices that help you slow down, notice what’s happening in your body and respond with care.

Often helpful for: Anxiety · Sleep · Overwhelm
