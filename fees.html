Insurance

Using your

insurance.

Out-of-network benefits

Many plans reimburse part of the cost of seeing an out-of-network therapist. We’ll give you what you need to claim.

Superbills on request

We provide a detailed receipt (a superbill) that you can submit to your insurer for reimbursement.

Check your coverage

Ask your insurer about out-of-network mental health benefits, your deductible and how to submit claims.

HSA & FSA cards welcome

You can usually use a health savings or flexible spending account to pay for sessions.

Good to know

Payments &

policies.

The practical details, all in one place.

More fee questions

01

When do I pay?

Payment is taken securely by card after each session, and we’ll email you a receipt.

02

What is your cancellation policy?

We ask for at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations may be charged the full session fee.

03

Do online sessions cost the same?

Yes. Online and in-person sessions are the same price and with the same licensed therapists.

04

Can I get a receipt for my records?

Yes. You’ll receive a receipt after every session, and a superbill whenever you need one for insurance.
