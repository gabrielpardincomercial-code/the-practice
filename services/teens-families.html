Format

In person & online

We usually begin with a parent or caregiver conversation, followed by time with your teen on their own. This helps us understand the full picture while giving your young person their own space.

Balancing privacy and involvement

Teens need to trust that therapy is theirs. We’ll agree together on what’s shared with parents, while always keeping you informed about safety and overall progress.

Bringing the family in

When it’s helpful, we invite family members into sessions to practise listening, set expectations and reconnect.

Our approach

How

we

work

together.

Family systems therapy

Looking at how the whole family interacts, so change doesn’t rest on one person’s shoulders.

CBT for adolescents

Age-appropriate tools for managing worries, moods and stressful situations.

Solution-focused therapy

Building on what’s already working to create realistic, hopeful next steps.

Questions

Good

to

know.

Still wondering about something? Ask us anything during your free consultation.

What ages do you work with?

We work with young people from 13 upwards, as well as with parents, caregivers and siblings in family sessions.

What if my teen doesn’t want to come?

That’s okay. Many parents start with a consultation on their own, and we can talk through gentle ways to involve your teen.

Will you tell me what my teen says?

We keep general progress and any safety concerns open with parents, while protecting the day-to-day details your teen shares.
