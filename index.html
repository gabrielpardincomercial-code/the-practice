How it works

Getting started,

gently.

Reaching out is often the hardest part. We’ve kept everything after that simple, clear and at your pace.

Book a free consultation

01

Reach out

Book a free consultation online or send us a note. No referral needed — just tell us a little about what’s on your mind.

02

Get matched

We’ll listen to what you’re looking for and pair you with a therapist whose experience and style feel right for you.

03

Begin your sessions

Meet in person or online, at a rhythm that fits your life. Early sessions are simply about getting to know you.

04

Grow at your pace

Check in on how it’s going, adjust as life changes and keep moving toward feeling more like yourself.
